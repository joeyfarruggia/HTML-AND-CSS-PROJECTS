How we got started!

This is a passion project that my wife and I came up with.
We love having good quality, long lasting products, that not only look good, but perform good as well.
So I came up with this design for a suppressor cover thinking that I wanted something that was a different vibe than the tactical option everyone else sells.
So what we have created is a product that I hope you love as much as I do, and plan to keep it forever, and pass it down to your kids, like I plan to do with mine.

Why we use the products we chose

In searching for a product that was both nice to look at, and something that was safe to use, I decided to take a look at natural fibers.
Wool is our main cover option, and is actually heat resistant to roughly 1000 degrees F. It is also self extinguishing. So what better fabric to chose, that can have great color patterns as well.
We use a high temp rated fiberglass woven material inside, and finished the edges with leather binding, and leather straps to hold it in place.
